Alerts

Fired when a condition starts: a stablecoin more than 0.5% off $1, an asset more than 1% from its outside price, a token moving more than 5% in an hour, or a cross-venue route clearing its costs. Pools under $20K are ignored.

Best execution

Perpetuals

Every perp market on Rocky and Ekiden. Basis is the perp price over the outside spot price (CoinGecko for BTC and ETH; gold and silver against Ekiden's spot index; CC against its Canton price). Ekiden basis uses its mark price.

Method
Tokens are matched by Canton instrument (issuer party and id); Pool Party publishes no issuer, so its tokens match by instrument id. Cantex and Tradecraft are priced from live reserves with each venue's own formula, OneSwap and Pool Party from reserves as constant-product pools (their fee and curve as documented or, for Pool Party, as CCTools reports), Temple and Rocky from their order books. A token's Canton price weighs each venue by the dollars it can trade within 1% of its own mid, and so does Canton Coin's, across every CC/USDCx pool and Temple's CC/USDCx order book (since 14 Oct 2026; before, the pools alone). For an order book, liquidity is the dollars resting within 1% of mid. A 24h or 7d change needs a price from the last two hours; a market that stopped trading shows none.

    
Data
Temple's API (account key), and the Cantex, Tradecraft, OneSwap, Pool Party, Rocky and Ekiden public APIs, DefiLlama for Canton's TVL, CoinGecko for outside prices and logos, Ekiden's index for gold and silver spot. Temple's volume comes from its own public settled-volume route.
